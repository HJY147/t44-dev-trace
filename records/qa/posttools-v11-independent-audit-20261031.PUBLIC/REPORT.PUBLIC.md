## Limits

This evidence does not show 400 concurrent real connections finish complete large histories within 40 seconds, actual throughput/P95/error results, deployment readiness, MQ delivery, or final business acceptance. It does not exercise Linux cgroup/rlimit limits, pool resource exhaustion, killed/stuck processes, disk-full/corrupt seal handling, active-reader cancellation, supervisor hard kill, or every full-execution exception branch. Spawn-pool close/join is not itself time-bounded; hard ownership supervisor remains the ultimate process bound. Nonzero sealing is demonstrated for an ordinary failure record, not an unconditional guarantee under abrupt kill or I/O failure. No neutral replay file was needed/read; schema differential coverage uses independent synthetic fixtures. No historical result was edited. No private key/credential was read or published.
