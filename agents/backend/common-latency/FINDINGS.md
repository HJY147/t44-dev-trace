## Fail-open correction after reviewer counterexample

Initial6bb dispatch-clock access occurred before cleanup try/finally and span-finally/export errors could mask business results, including a successful commit. These were real observer defects, not production latency evidence. Updated instrumentation guards only its own clock/count/export faults; business invocation remains outside those exception guards. Token reset covers all post-set paths with prior-context fallback. Capture reads optional dispatch timestamp only when enabled. Span finally cannot replace a business exception with a diagnostic failure. Seven diagnostic checks pass including fault injection, exception identity, context cleanup, parallel isolation, metric/header bounds and realHTTP defaultoff with optionalclock deliberately failing.64metric/2048character bounds; no SQL/user input metrics. NativeHTTP and artificial wait calibration are delegated to DBowner, not inferred from SQLite. All observer code remains ordinary production LOC.
